About this event
----------------

**Introducing Code for Communities 2.0 a next-generation hackathon that brings together developers, innovators, AI enthusiasts, and builders to solve real-world challenges through technology and Artificial Intelligence.**

Organized by **GDG Bhubaneswar and GDG Cloud Bhubaneswar** as part of Google's **Build with AI** initiative, Code for Communities 2.0 provides a platform for developers, startup enthusiasts, students, researchers, and technology professionals to collaborate, experiment, and build innovative solutions with the potential to create meaningful real-world impact.

The hackathon is centered around solving practical challenges through **AI-powered technology, creative problem-solving, and scalable innovation**. Participants will have the opportunity to work in teams, explore modern AI and Google Cloud technologies, and transform their ideas into solutions designed for real-world use.

This is more than just a coding competition. **Code for Communities 2.0 is an opportunity to build technology with purpose** bringing together technical skills, community-driven innovation, and a focus on solving challenges that matter.  
  
**Prize Pool**  
• Total prize pool: **₹10 lakh**

### **Problem Statements**

**1. AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance**  
Leverage Artificial Intelligence to improve public services, governance systems, citizen experiences, and digital infrastructure.

**2. Clean Air & Climate Resilience**  
Build innovative solutions that use AI and technology to address air pollution, environmental challenges, climate risks, and community resilience.

**3. Smart Health & Supply Chain Resilience**  
Develop intelligent solutions for healthcare delivery, health systems, resource management, and resilient supply chains.

**4. Agricultural Intelligence**  
Use AI and technology to empower farmers, improve agricultural decision-making, enhance productivity, and build smarter agricultural ecosystems.

### **What to Expect**

* Real-world problem statements designed to encourage meaningful innovation
* Opportunities to build AI-powered and technology-driven solutions
* Hands-on exploration of Google Cloud and AI technologies
* Collaboration with developers, designers, students, founders, and technology enthusiasts
* Mentorship, knowledge sharing, and community-driven learning
* Opportunities to showcase your solution and compete with innovative teams from across the country
* A platform to transform ideas into practical solutions with real-world potential

### **Who Should Participate?**

* Software Developers
* AI/ML Enthusiasts
* Cloud Engineers
* Students and Researchers
* Startup Founders and Builders
* Designers and Product Innovators
* Technology Professionals
* Anyone passionate about using technology and AI to solve meaningful problems

Whether you're an experienced developer building production-ready AI applications or someone taking your first steps into the world of Artificial Intelligence, **Code for Communities 2.0** gives you an opportunity to learn, collaborate, experiment, and build alongside a vibrant community of innovators.

**Build with AI. Build for communities. Build solutions that matter.**

**Join us to learn together, build together, and elevate the community.**
